Good to know

Quick answers.

Is the quote really free?

Yes. The visit and the written quote don’t cost you anything.

Do you need to visit first?

For ducted systems, always. For a simple split, we can often estimate from photos, then confirm the price when we see the place.

How long is the quote good for?

30 days. If equipment prices change, we’ll let you know.

Can I choose my own brand?

Of course. We’ll make a recommendation, and the final choice is yours.

I’m renting. Can I still get one?

With your landlord’s written permission, yes. We can give you a one-page summary to share with them.

I live in an apartment.

You’ll need strata approval, and we’re happy to help prepare the application.
