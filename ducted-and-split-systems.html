Ducted air conditioning

Whole-home comfort, out of sight.

A ducted system treats your whole home from one central unit. Air travels through insulated ducts to slim outlets in the ceiling, so you get even temperatures without wall units in every room.

Zoning that saves money

Split the home into zones, such as living, bedrooms and study, and only run the areas you’re using. A wall controller, or the app on your phone, lets you set each zone’s temperature.

Split system air conditioning

Why a split suits most single rooms.

A split system pairs an indoor wall unit with an outdoor unit. It’s the most cost-effective way to cool or heat one space, and the technology is mature and efficient. Most models today are inverter reverse-cycle, so they cool in summer and heat in winter at a fraction of the running cost of a bar heater.

Multi-split

One outdoor unit can run two to five indoor units, which helps when you don’t have space for a lot of outdoor units. We’ll tell you if it’s better value than separate systems.

Right-sizing a split

The most common mistake is buying a system that’s too big. We always size to the room you’re cooling.

Running costs

What will it cost to run?

Roughly, a well-sized inverter system costs about 20 to 60 cents an hour to run for a typical room on a mild day, and more on a very hot or cold one.

Costs are estimates and vary with your electricity tariff and usage.

  • How well your home is insulated and sealed
  • The efficiency rating (look for high star ratings for both cooling and heating)
  • Your thermostat setting. Each degree matters.
  • Whether you zone or run the whole house
  • Regular filter cleaning

How long does a ducted system last?

Typically 15 to 20 years with regular servicing. Splits average 10 to 15.

Will ducted work in a single-storey home with a small roof space?

Often, yes. We’ll check the roof space on the site visit, and if it’s too tight we’ll recommend alternatives.

Is ducted noisy?

No. The fan and compressor are in the roof or outside, so you mainly hear air movement.

Can I add zones later?

Usually yes, if planned at the time. Tell us in advance.

Do I need council approval?

Generally not for houses. For apartments, strata approval is needed, and we can help prepare it.

What brand is best?

There is no single best brand. Each has strengths. We’ll compare two or three that suit you.
