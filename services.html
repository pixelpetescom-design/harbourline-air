Installation

New installations

Thinking about your first system? We’ll pop round, measure up, and suggest the simplest setup that keeps every room comfortable. We install ducted, split, multi-split and cassette systems in houses, townhouses, apartments and offices.

What’s included: site assessment, load calculation, supply of equipment, all electrical work, pipework, drainage, commissioning and a full walk-through.

Replacing an old system

Replacing a tired system is usually quicker and cheaper than a first install, because the pipework and electrical are often reusable. We’ll tell you honestly if yours can be reused and what that saves.

Typical timeline: one day for a split, two to three days for ducted.

Common questions

Good to know.

Do you charge for quotes?

No. Quotes and site visits are free in all our service areas.

Can you install a system I’ve bought elsewhere?

Yes, for a labour-only price. The workmanship warranty applies, but the manufacturer’s equipment warranty is between you and the supplier.

Do you service systems you didn’t install?

Yes, most major brands.

How soon can you start?

Quotes are usually booked within three working days. Installs typically start within two to three weeks of accepting. In peak summer, book early.

Not sure where to start?

Tell us about your home and we’ll point you in the right direction. No obligation, no pressure.
