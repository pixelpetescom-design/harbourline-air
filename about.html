Hi, we’re Harbourline. Nice to meet you.

We started in 2008 with two technicians and one van in Marrickville. We’re still a Sydney business, and we still sweat the small stuff: straight pipework, tidy ducting and a clean floor when we leave.

Our story

Tom and Rachel started it.

Tom and Rachel (our founders) spent years working for other firms and kept seeing the same things. Systems that were too big for the room. Pipes run badly. Customers who weren’t sure what they’d paid for.

So they set up a company that tells customers the truth and does installs properly.

Eighteen years later, we’re a team of 26 installers, service technicians and office staff. Most of our work comes from repeat customers and referrals, which is the best compliment we could ask for. And yes, we still say no to jobs if we don’t think they’re right for you.
